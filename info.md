Track your anti-mosquito diffuser cartridge from the smart plug it's plugged into.

- Cartridge usage counted from the outlet's on/off state only
- One-press "new cartridge" reset
- Remaining hours, percent, and days left at your real usage pace
- Optional built-in on/off schedule with pause switch and month (season) selection
- Low-cartridge warning binary sensor
- Bundled Lovelace card: gauge, days left, used/left hours bar and one-tap actions
- Multiple diffusers supported (one config entry each)
